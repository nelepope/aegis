A fingerprint pendant is a linking object to someone you love.

When you touch it, you're activating the same neural pathways that create feelings of connection. You're not just remembering them—you’re carrying their presence with you always, like a tiny love note you can carry everywhere.

HOW DOES BESPOKE WORK?

1. On purchasing, you’ll receive an invitation to book an appointment with me in my studio in Camden, London, where we create a fingerprint impression in wax.

2. The wax is then cast in a metal of your choice, and the resulting cast is turned into a pendant before being refined and polished.

3. Your pendant will be packaged with love and delivered safely to your door.

HOW DOES BESPOKE WORK?

You / the person with the respective fingerprint, book a 30-minute appointment with me in my studio. The tip of the finger is pressed into a ball of soft red (jewellers') wax. A few impressions may be taken, and we will select the best one for casting.

The wax is then cast in a metal of your choice, and the resulting cast is turned into a pendant before being refined and polished.

From the appointment date, pendants typically take 3 weeks to be ready for shipping to you.
